## Audit interpretation

- The book supplies **questions and strategic arguments**, not empirical pass probabilities.
- Only some ideas translate directly into testable controls: distribution economics, customer segments, risk of fake uniqueness, and focus on sustainable advantage.
- Original 2012 lecture notes strengthen distribution and secrecy context but do **not** supersede the published book.
- The most important missing procedural topics for 021 are customer discovery, disciplined assumptions, reproducible builds, launch readiness, first-customer operations, revenue verification and cost of AI supervision.
- No single case (PayPal, Tesla, Apple, Facebook) demonstrates that adopting the framework causes success.
